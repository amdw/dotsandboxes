Dots-and-Boxes

Material on the game of dots-and-boxes, and its close relative strings-and-coins.

  • Engine

    Play dots-and-boxes or strings-and-coins, against the engine or yourself, from any position. See the value of every move, Nimstring values, and canonical moves.

    A draft paper on the theory of the game (PDF).
